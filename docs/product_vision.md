# The Macro Narrative Engine

## Product Vision

*This document explains why MNE exists. Every other document in this repository — the Source Intelligence Platform, the Historical Replay Engine, the Narrative Memory System, the Intelligence Experience Architecture, the Platform Observability Layer, the Research Workspace Architecture — explains how MNE works. This one should still be true if every line of that work were rewritten from scratch.*

---

## 1. Why MNE Exists

Markets do not suffer from a shortage of information. They are drowning in it. Every day produces more headlines, more data releases, more commentary, more noise than any person could read, let alone make sense of. What markets are actually short on is *understanding* — a clear, honest account of what story is actually being told, why it's being told now, and what actually supports it.

MNE exists to close that gap. Not by adding to the noise, and not by guessing at what comes next, but by building a disciplined, evidence-based account of the narratives already moving through the market — where they came from, how they're evolving, and how well they're actually supported by what's real.

---

## 2. Mission

**To help people understand markets by understanding the narratives that move them — clearly, honestly, and with evidence.**

---

## 3. Long-Term Vision

MNE is becoming an **evidence-driven research operating system for understanding market narratives.**

It is not an RSS reader. It is not a dashboard. It is not an AI application, a chatbot, or a news summarizer. Those are shapes MNE might temporarily wear on the way to something larger — a foundational layer of infrastructure for narrative understanding, one that other tools, workflows, and eventually other systems can be built on top of. In its fullest form, MNE is where a person goes not to be told what to think about the market, but to actually investigate it — with every conclusion traceable, every piece of evidence inspectable, and every year of history preserved and re-examinable exactly as it was understood at the time.

---

## 4. The Problem MNE Solves

Markets generate an enormous, continuous volume of information — headlines, filings, data releases, commentary — and almost none of it arrives pre-organized into anything resembling understanding. A person trying to follow a single narrative thread today must manually assemble it from dozens of disconnected sources, with no reliable way to know how strong the underlying evidence actually is, or how the story has changed over time.

MNE exists to do that organizing work — deterministically, transparently, and continuously — turning raw information into structured narrative intelligence a person can actually reason with.

---

## 5. Who MNE Is For

MNE is built for anyone trying to genuinely understand what's moving markets — traders, investors, researchers, students, analysts, and institutions alike. What unites them is not their skill level or their role, but a shared need: the need to investigate, to ask *why*, and to see the evidence behind an answer rather than simply being handed one.

A first-time investor trying to understand today's market and an institutional researcher reconstructing a narrative's history over the past five years are doing the same fundamental thing — investigating — at different depths. MNE is built for that shared act of investigation, not for any one segment of people who happen to do it professionally.

---

## 6. Product Philosophy

- **Evidence before opinion.**
- **Understanding before prediction.**
- **Investigation before conclusions.**
- **Deterministic intelligence before AI.**
- **Transparency before automation.**

Each of these orderings is deliberate. It would be easy, and often tempting, to build the second half of each pair first — opinions are more entertaining than evidence, predictions feel more valuable than understanding, conclusions are faster to deliver than investigation, AI is quicker to build with than deterministic logic, and automation is more impressive than transparency. MNE is built in the harder order, on purpose, because the harder order is the only one that produces something genuinely trustworthy.

---

## 7. Product Principles

- **Every conclusion should be explainable.** If MNE cannot say why it believes something, it should not say it.
- **Evidence should remain traceable.** A user should always be able to follow any conclusion back to the specific evidence that produced it.
- **AI should assist, never replace.** AI makes MNE's intelligence more accessible; it never becomes the source of that intelligence.
- **Architecture should remain modular.** Each part of MNE should be able to improve independently, without the whole needing to be rebuilt.
- **History should remain reconstructable.** What MNE understood on any given day should remain knowable, faithfully, forever.
- **Research should remain reproducible.** The same question, asked against the same evidence, should always yield the same answer.
- **Users should always understand where intelligence came from.** Not just what MNE concluded, but why, and from what.

---

## 8. What Makes MNE Different

MNE is not trying to replace Bloomberg, ChatGPT, TradingView, or traditional financial news. Each of those occupies a category MNE has no interest in competing in — real-time market data terminals, general-purpose conversational AI, charting platforms, news reporting. MNE occupies a different category entirely: **an evidence-driven intelligence and research platform**, built specifically to answer a question none of those tools are built to answer — not "what happened" or "what's the price" or "what does an AI think," but *what story is actually being told, how well is it actually supported, and how did it get here.*

---

## 9. What MNE Is Not

To be unambiguous: MNE is not an automated trading system. It is not a prediction engine. It is not a recommendation engine. It is not an opinion generator. It is not an AI decision maker.

MNE does not tell anyone what to buy, what to sell, or what will happen next. It tells people what is actually happening in the narrative landscape of the market, how confident it is in that read, and why — and then gets out of the way of the human being who has to decide what to do with that understanding.

---

## 10. The Role of AI

AI has a real, permanent role inside MNE — but it is always the role of an assistant operating on intelligence that already exists, never the role of the intelligence itself. This is governed, in full technical detail, by the Intelligence Experience Architecture; the principle stated plainly here is the one that document exists to protect.

AI may explain, summarize, compare, teach, personalize how information is presented, and help organize an investigation. AI must never generate deterministic intelligence, modify deterministic intelligence, override evidence, or replace investigation. Wherever AI makes MNE feel more like a conversation and less like a spreadsheet, that is AI doing its proper job. The moment AI would need to conclude something MNE's deterministic systems haven't already concluded, it has stepped outside that job — and MNE does not let it.

---

## 11. The Future of MNE

MNE's future is not a list of features. It is the continued deepening of a single idea: that markets can be understood, not just watched, if the evidence behind their narratives is organized honestly and preserved faithfully over time.

That future connects Narrative Memory, Historical Replay, Company Memory, the Research Workspace, and an AI Research Assistant into a single, coherent research environment — one where understanding today's market and understanding how markets have narratively evolved over years are the same underlying capability, just pointed at different points in time. Every one of these systems is being built to extend MNE's reach without ever changing what MNE fundamentally is. Whatever MNE becomes next, it will still be evidence before opinion, understanding before prediction, and investigation before conclusions.

---

## 12. Success Criteria

MNE's success is not measured by user count, revenue, or popularity. Those may follow, but they are not the point, and a version of MNE that had all three while quietly abandoning its principles would be a failure by the only measure that actually matters here.

**MNE succeeds when the people who use it understand markets better than they otherwise could have.** That is the entire test. Every feature, every architectural decision, every future direction should be measured against it — not "does this make MNE more impressive," but "does this make understanding more possible."

---

## 13. Guiding Decisions

When a future decision is unclear, it should be resolved by asking, in order:

1. Does this make MNE's intelligence more explainable, or less?
2. Does this make evidence more traceable, or less?
3. Does this bring users closer to genuine investigation, or does it hand them a conclusion instead?
4. Does this preserve MNE's deterministic core, or does it quietly outsource judgment to something less accountable?
5. Would this decision still make sense to defend in five years, or does it only make sense as a shortcut for today?

A decision that fails any of these questions should be reconsidered, regardless of how compelling it looks in the moment — speed, novelty, and competitive pressure are not reasons to compromise on any of the five.

---

## 14. Closing Philosophy

MNE exists because understanding a market's story should not require guesswork, and because trusting a conclusion should never require blind faith. Every narrative MNE describes rests on evidence that can be inspected. Every conclusion MNE reaches can be explained. Every day MNE has ever understood the market remains available to be understood again.

This is what MNE is, and what it will remain, no matter how much of it is eventually rebuilt: a place where the story of the market is told honestly, traced back to what's real, and never mistaken for a prediction of what comes next.
