# Narrative Sector Model

Version 1.0.0 is a curated, deterministic map from canonical narrative groups to canonical sectors. Each stored record contains a sector key, one structural role (`PRIMARY`, `SECONDARY`, `EMERGING`, `OFFSET`, or `DETACHED`), an optional expected expression (`UP` or `DOWN`), a rationale, and a display flag. Omitted sectors have no curated relationship; omission does not mean detached. Geopolitical Risk remains intentionally unmapped.

The read model adds `participation_state`, `evidence_count`, `instruments`, explanation text, availability, and a route link. Structural role describes a durable connection in MNE's narrative model. Participation describes current sector-level market behavior. They are separate fields and separate visual signals.

Participation supports `STRONG`, `PARTICIPATING`, `EMERGING`, `MIXED`, `DETACHED`, `CONTRADICTING`, and `UNAVAILABLE`. The live run fetches one curated ETF per sector through the existing `get_market_snapshot` path and persists each result in the existing flat snapshot under its sector key. `config/sector_instruments.json` is the canonical sector-to-ETF registry. One ETF is a sampling proxy, not exhaustive sector coverage. `MIXED` is architecture-ready but unreachable while each sector has only one input.

Classification fails closed when the registry entry, persisted record, timestamp, percentage move, or expected expression is missing. A fresh move aligned with the curated direction is `STRONG`, `PARTICIPATING`, or `EMERGING` according to named 1.0% and 0.25% boundaries; an emerging structural role is capped at emerging participation. A meaningful opposing move in a primary or secondary sector is `CONTRADICTING`. Near-zero or otherwise non-meaningful fresh movement is `DETACHED`. QQQ, NVDA, VIX, DXY, narrative-level Market Expression, structural role, and headline evidence are never used as substitutes.

Freshness is separate from participation. `observed_at` is required, data older than the named 36-hour `SECTOR_STALE_MAX_AGE` is `STALE`, and stale records are excluded from classification. There is no exchange-calendar support yet, so Friday data can become stale over a weekend and holidays can create the same honest unavailable state; the model does not widen the window or pretend that aged data is current.

Structural breadth counts curated sector connections and their role mix. Participation breadth counts only fresh, classifiable observations and reports contradicted, broad, moderate, concentrated, limited, or unavailable. Its 3/2/1 breakpoints and the emerging-role ceiling are first-pass rules to revisit after real observations accumulate. Old persisted runs have no sector keys or timestamps and continue to render participation as unavailable without migration or backfill.
