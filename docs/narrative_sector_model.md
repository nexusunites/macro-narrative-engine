# Narrative Sector Model

Version 1.0.0 is a curated, deterministic map from canonical narrative groups to canonical sectors. Each stored record contains a sector key, one structural role (`PRIMARY`, `SECONDARY`, `EMERGING`, `OFFSET`, or `DETACHED`), an optional expected expression (`UP` or `DOWN`), a rationale, and a display flag. Omitted sectors have no curated relationship; omission does not mean detached. Geopolitical Risk remains intentionally unmapped.

The read model adds `participation_state`, `evidence_count`, `instruments`, explanation text, availability, and a route link. Structural role describes a durable connection in MNE's narrative model. Participation describes current sector-level market behavior. They are separate fields and separate visual signals.

Participation supports `STRONG`, `PARTICIPATING`, `EMERGING`, `MIXED`, `DETACHED`, `CONTRADICTING`, and `UNAVAILABLE`. The live run fetches one curated ETF per sector through the existing `get_market_snapshot` path and persists each result in the existing flat snapshot under its sector key. `config/sector_instruments.json` is the canonical sector-to-ETF registry. One ETF is a sampling proxy, not exhaustive sector coverage. `MIXED` is architecture-ready but unreachable while each sector has only one input.

Classification fails closed when the registry entry, persisted record, timestamp, percentage move, or expected expression is missing. A fresh move aligned with the curated direction is `STRONG`, `PARTICIPATING`, or `EMERGING` according to named 1.0% and 0.25% boundaries. Structural role does not cap participation. A meaningful opposing move in any mapped sector with an explicit expected expression is `CONTRADICTING`. Near-zero or otherwise non-meaningful fresh movement is `DETACHED`. QQQ, NVDA, VIX, DXY, narrative-level Market Expression, structural role, and headline evidence are never used as substitutes.

Freshness is separate from participation. `observed_at` is required. Freshness counts completed NYSE sessions, so Friday-close data remains fresh through the weekend and a pre-holiday close remains fresh through the closure. After the next session close, an eight-hour grace window allows the daily pipeline to persist the new close; after that window the prior observation is stale, and two completed sessions always make it stale. Future, missing, and malformed timestamps fail closed to unavailable. A ten-day absolute backstop is defensive only. The calendar library handles exchange holidays and early closes; if it cannot be resolved, the conservative fallback is unavailable rather than guessed fresh.

Structural breadth counts curated sector connections and their role mix. Participation breadth counts only fresh, classifiable observations and reports contradicted, broad, moderate, concentrated, limited, or unavailable. Its provisional 3/2/1 breakpoints remain unchanged until at least 20 trading days (approximately 220 valid sector observations) have accumulated. Old persisted runs have no sector keys or timestamps and continue to render participation as unavailable without migration or backfill.
